Teammate guide

Creative Lens in 2 minutes.

Search live Meta ads, and see the well-designed ones first.

What it does

Pick an industry (or type any search). Creative Lens collects the image ads running in that market on Meta's public Ad Library, then an AI in your browser scores how designed each one looks and shows the best first. Copies of the same design and ads that aren't about your search are hidden.

One-time setup

You need Google Chrome or Brave on a laptop or desktop (in Brave, the page is brave://extensions instead of chrome://extensions). Microsoft Edge should work the same way (edge://extensions) but hasn't been tried yet. Firefox, Safari and phones can't run the helper. The website searches through a small Chrome add-on (the “Ad Snob helper”), so each search comes from your own browser: Meta blocks searches from servers. The helper searches logged out; it never uses your Facebook account.

  1. On the Creative Lens page, click ⬇ Download the helper (or download it here).
  2. Right-click the downloaded ad-snob-helper.zip → Extract All… → Extract. Keep the folder somewhere safe, like Documents: Chrome uses it from there, so don't delete it.
  3. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the ad-snob-helper folder that has manifest.json inside. (Extract All often makes a folder inside a folder: pick the inner one.)
  5. Go back to Creative Lens and press Ctrl+F5. The setup box disappears.

Chrome may say the add-on is in “developer mode”. That's expected: it isn't on the Chrome Web Store, it's our own.

Searching

  1. Use a mobile hotspot or home Wi-Fi. Office Wi-Fi blocks Facebook, so searches fail there.
  2. Click an industry on the left, or type a search and press Search.
  3. Ads appear as they're collected, then get scored. When everything is scored, the best move to the top. A full search can take 10–30 minutes; press Stop any time and you keep what was found.

The first search downloads the AI once (about 180 MB). Your first typed search (not an industry button) downloads one more part (about 127 MB). After that it's instant.

Please don't hammer the Search button. If Meta says it's limiting you, wait 10–15 minutes. Searching again and again makes the block last longer.

Reading the results

Design score
How designed the ad looks (layout, graphics, text), 0–100%.
✳ Design-led
Scores 50% or more. On by default; turn it off to see everything.
🔥 Likely winner
Running 3+ months in 3+ separate ads: the brand keeps paying for it. A clue, not proof.
🆕 New
Started in the last 2 weeks: something they're testing now.
Duplicate
Another size or crop of a design shown elsewhere. Hidden unless you press Show duplicates.
Off-topic
Neither the picture nor the ad's text is about your search (e.g. a hiring poster in “food”). Hidden unless you press Show off-topic.

Click any ad for details: all its versions, how long it has run, what the AI noticed, the ad text, and a link to the ad on Meta.

Saving ads

Press ♧ on an ad (or Save in its details) and it appears under Saved ads on the left, even after you close the page. Saved ads are kept in your own browser only: teammates don't see yours, and clearing Chrome's browsing data removes them.

If something goes wrong

The setup box won't go away
Check the helper is switched on in chrome://extensions, then press Ctrl+F5 here.
“Extension context invalidated”
The helper was reloaded or updated: press Ctrl+F5 on this page.
“Your helper is out of date”
In chrome://extensions, click Remove on Ad Snob helper, then do the setup again with a fresh download. Saved ads stay.
0 ads, or “Meta stopped responding”
You're probably on office Wi-Fi, or Meta is limiting your connection. Switch to a hotspot, or wait 10–15 minutes.
Some ads say “Couldn't score”
Their picture couldn't be downloaded. The rest are fine.

Still stuck? Open the Diagnostics section at the bottom of the page and send its text to whoever shared Creative Lens with you.

Good to know

  • It reads Meta's public Ad Library pages, like you would by hand. Use it for research, at a normal pace.
  • Nothing you search or save is sent to us: it all happens in your browser.
