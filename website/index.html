One-time setup (2 minutes): add the Ad Snob helper to Chrome

The website searches Meta through a small Chrome add-on, so each search comes from your own browser (Meta blocks searches from servers). It searches logged out: it never uses your Facebook account.

⬇ Download the helper
  1. Open the downloaded ad-snob-helper.zip: right-click it → Extract All… → Extract. Keep the folder somewhere safe (for example Documents): Chrome uses it from there.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (paste it into the address bar) and switch on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the ad-snob-helper folder that has manifest.json inside (after Extract All it's often one folder deeper).
  4. Come back here and press Ctrl+F5.

Updating: in chrome://extensions click Remove on the old “Ad Snob helper”, then do the 4 steps above with the new download. Your saved ads stay (they live in the website, not in the add-on).
